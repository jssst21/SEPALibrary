Welcome Aboard!

Thank you so much for joining us

The gift of your time helps more than 50 displaced families every month across the Philadelphia region.

2

Receive a DAT Induction

This is a face-to-face meeting with your Team Leader that gives you a high-level view of what we do and what the challenges can be. Working as a DAT responder is incredibly rewarding, but the 24/7/365 nature of the work doesn't fit every schedule. The Induction is your chance to ask whatever you need to make sure this is the right fit for you.

3

Get in the field

No two emergency responses are alike, so this is very much a learn-by-doing role. The best thing you can do after your Induction is dive right in.

  • Head over to your EDGE trainings and start your assigned courses. It can be hard to find these on the Volunteer Connection site, so use How to Find Your Assigned Trainings.
  • Begin with RC Respond Basics. It covers the ins and outs of going on call and gets you into the field to start learning what we do.
  • Tell your Team Leader when you have finished RC Respond Basics and you're ready to go out and observe. You'll be sent a link to set up your account. Then list yourself as "On Call" with the Noggin app on your phone or the website.
  • Requests for help will now start coming to your phone. Take a look at What a Request Looks Like on Your Phone to see exactly what will be coming to you and understand each step.
4

Further course work

  • One course must be taken in person by every new responder: DAT Response Fundamentals. It's offered every month. Find it here, along with other classes and events you may be interested in. Just click an event and hit "Register".
  • Keep working through the rest of your online courses.
5

Your first time out – Don't panic!

You're not supposed to know what to do. There will always be a supervisor with you. You're there to listen, learn, and ask the supervisor every question you have.

If someone we're helping comes to you, it's perfectly OK to say:

"I'm a new volunteer, but I'll make sure the supervisor comes over to help you. Can I get you some water or a blanket right now?"

The guides below, like Your First Time to a Scene, describe what to expect at a scene. Give them a read, but it's OK if you don't remember everything. Your supervisor will guide you.
